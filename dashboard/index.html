How the implied value is worked out

Every row's implied value is tvl_usd × category multiple × growth multiplier × ripeness multiplier × discovery multiplier. Nothing is hand-picked per protocol: the same formula runs over every row in the snapshot.

Why a bell curve over age, not "older is always better"

A protocol with no track record is a protocol you can't underwrite — there's no history to show it survives a drawdown. A protocol that has run for years with still no token is also past its highest odds: the market has largely priced in "no airdrop is coming". The ripeness curve peaks in between, rewarding protocols old enough to trust and young enough that a launch is still plausible.
